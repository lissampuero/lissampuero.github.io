Character Universe I · Marquise of Croac

A Letter from the Swamp

Ahh— I see you're captivated by my magnificence. You have proven yourself worthy of my greatness. Very well… continue.

My darlings,

Allow me the pleasure, or the misfortune, depending on your mediocre luck, of introducing myself: I am The Marquise of Croac, amphibian aristocrat. Who needs jewels when one already shines this brightly?

Fireflies and mud, and the entire swamp is my ballroom. Living chandeliers, toads singing in chorus just for me. The waters seem clearer when they reflect my image. Born with false lashes, and scandal with impeccable posture.

My life is a perpetual parade: impossible gowns, gravity-defying hairstyles, and phrases that, frankly, should be embroidered on palace walls… if only there were a palace worthy of me.

So if you ever dare to enter my domain, remember to bow, clumsily will do, and prepare your ears: because my voice, sweet, sharp, unforgettable, will always echo louder than yours.

Sincerely,
in purple ink and lily perfume

Character Universe I · Marquise of Croac

Character Highlight

So. You wish to know what makes me… magnificent. Wise choice. Read carefully — there will be no repeating.

Species. Amphibian aristocrat, self-proclaimed royalty of the swamp.

Residence. A lily-pad palace, adorned with dragonflies and scented with algae.

Personality. Sarcastic, dramatic, vain, witty, delightfully unbearable.

Notable quotes

  • “The swamp is my stage, and I am the only protagonist.”
  • “Darling, if you can’t shine, at least make noise when you fall.”
  • “If you need me, I’ll be busy being unforgettable.”
  • “The loudest croak will always be mine.”
  • “Modesty is a luxury I simply won’t afford.”

Strengths

Turns mud into a stage. Can silence an entire swamp with one raised eyebrow. Unshakable self-love, and an impressive collection of wigs.

Weaknesses

Cannot tolerate mediocrity. Prone to fainting if applause is insufficient.

Legacy

The Marquise of Croac is not just a frog; she is the event. A living satire wrapped in silk, a reminder that even in the muddiest of swamps, one can reign with style, sarcasm, and unapologetic vanity.

Character Universe I · Marquise of Croac

Memoirs of the Marquise of Croac

Ahh, my memoirs. Few are permitted this close to greatness. Turn the page, darling — if you can keep up.

“Dearest creatures of muddy waters and fragile wings, I bestow upon you the honour of my memoirs. A legacy written in fine mud and perfumed with water lilies, so you may learn from me and, with luck, love yourselves a little more. Because if I, with these regal legs and swamp complexion, consider myself divine… what excuse do you have?”

Eternal truths

  • “Mud only dirties if you don’t know how to strut on it.”
  • “Envy is nothing but a mosquito: small, annoying, and easily crushed with my fan.”
  • “One well-intoned croak can slay more than a thousand speeches.”
  • “I am never late. The swamp begins when I arrive.”
  • “If your reflection in the water doesn’t adore you, change ponds.”
  • “Humility is overrated; I prefer the honesty of my magnificence.”

Character Universe I · Marquise of Croac

The Swamp Court

You dare enter my court uninvited? Very well — since you are already here, kneel, and listen closely.

They call me extravagant, dramatic, unbearable… and I call it simply existing at a higher level of grace. I am the Marquise sovereign, fashion icon of the swamp, and the only reason mosquitoes bother to shine at night.

My palace? This swamp, decorated by dragonflies and perfumed by algae. My throne? A perfectly chosen lily pad, naturally.

Oh, the peasants croak, they jump, they splash… but someone must give glamour to this puddle, and who better than me?

“Applaud? Or do you croak out of habit?” I once asked my subjects. Silence. Because when the Marquise speaks, the swamp listens.

Respect? Oh no, worship will suffice.

Yes, I have ridden a fish as if it were a horse. Yes, I wear wigs taller than reeds and heavier than a frog’s conscience. And yes, everything looks fabulous on me, especially tragedy.

Remember my words, little tadpoles of mediocrity: nothing is eternal, not even the lily blossoms… except my elegance.

Character Universe II · LoLo & Fuzzle

Character Highlights

Yay, you came! Fuzzle says hi too — he's the fluffy one hiding behind the brush. Here's our story…

LoLo is a dream painter: every brushstroke opens a world, every dream becomes a destination. He lives with joy, innocence, and playful mischief, turning feelings into magical journeys. Fuzzle, his fluffy bird plush, is his witty, mischievous best friend and inseparable companion.

Their world is whimsical: rain becomes a playground, books become stairways, clouds turn into castles. Together, they show that imagination can paint reality with wonder.

Character Universe II · LoLo & Fuzzle

Character Sheet

Shhh, come in quietly — Fuzzle's still deciding if he trusts you. Okay, he says you can stay. Here's everything about us…

LoLo lives life with the joy and wonder of a child who never stops being amazed. For him, every day is a new adventure; every cloud looks like a castle, and every puddle is an ocean full of secrets. His greatest power lies in his imagination: he paints what he dreams and dreams what he paints. With his brushes, he turns feelings into journeys; if he misses someone, he paints hugs; if he feels curious, he paints doors to unknown worlds; if he wants to play, he paints friends that come alive.

By his side is always his inseparable companion: Fuzzle, a fluffy bird plush that, although soft and huggable, seems to have a mind of its own. Together they are a mischievous, inseparable duo: LoLo paints, and Fuzzle comments, observes, and sometimes sneaks his own silly ideas into the canvases.

LoLo’s world is full of innocent mischief: jumping in the rain until he feels like a frog, talking to butterflies only he can understand, or hiding in his grandmother’s mountain of books while imagining they are steps to another planet. With each brushstroke, he creates a portal, and with each dream, he finds a new destination.

LoLo doesn’t just paint worlds, he paints the way he wants to live them. Because for him, life is not a place to be, but an adventure to imagine.
