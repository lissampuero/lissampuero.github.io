AboutMe

My History

I'm Liss Ampuero, a digital artist, illustrator, and art director based in Belgium, working with clients worldwide. My process starts the same way every time: I listen to an idea, however small or unformed, and give it a voice, a face, a world to live in.

I'm passionate about creating cozy, whimsical characters and sharing storylines that spark emotion and give soul to the things we see. From books and brands to characters and packaging, I bring ideas to life with color, texture, and intention: playful but precise, intuitive but thoughtful, always with a touch of magic.

Versatility is my superpower. I shape my style to match each project's heart, whether it calls for elegance, boldness, softness, or a little rebellion, and I approach every project with the same care: capture the idea, shape it with intention, and deliver a finished universe, ready for digital use or print.

Magic is real, and it lives within you.
